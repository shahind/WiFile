# WiFile Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 28, 2026_

WiFile is a free app for sharing files and chatting with other computers on your local network. It works entirely offline.

## We don't collect anything

- WiFile does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.
- There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no telemetry.
- WiFile never connects to the internet or to any server run by us or anyone else.

## What stays on your local network

WiFile only talks to other devices on your own local network that are also running WiFile:

- **Your device name** (for example "Alex (DESKTOP-123)") is announced on the local network so other WiFile devices can find you. You can change this name in the app.
- **Files you put in the shared folder** are copied directly to the other WiFile devices on the network.
- **Chat messages and attachments** you send go directly to the devices you choose.

This data never leaves your local network, and it never passes through us. Anyone on the same network who runs WiFile can see the shared folder and your messages to everyone, so only use WiFile on networks you trust.

## What is stored on your computer

WiFile keeps a few files on your own computer so it can work: your shared folder, your chat history, received chat files, its settings and a technical log. They stay on your computer and are never sent to us. You can delete chat messages in the app, and uninstalling WiFile plus deleting these folders removes everything.

## Children

WiFile does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new date.
